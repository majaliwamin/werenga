Audio quality comparison

The same 3 minutes of Ar-Ra'd (recitation then Chichewa translation), encoded at different settings. This is what the bundled library uses.

Original MP3 · 128 kbps 2.9 MB / 3 min
The size the recordings had before conversion.
AAC 64 kbpsin use 1.5 MB / 3 min
Half the size, indistinguishable from the original for speech. All 36 recordings: 793 MB.
AAC 48 kbps 1.1 MB / 3 min
The alternative: 60% smaller, slightly duller consonants. All 36 recordings: about 600 MB.
AAC 40 kbps 0.9 MB / 3 min
Smallest. Fine on a phone speaker, noticeably duller on headphones. All 36 recordings: about 500 MB.
AAC plays on every Android version the app supports (7.0 and newer) and in Chrome. Ogg Opus would be smaller still, but needs Android 10+ unless the player bundles its own decoder, so it is not used.
